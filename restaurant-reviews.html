Featured long-form review

Is Gepuklah's Ayam Gepuk Worth the Craving?

Working title for Assignment 2. The finished article must be based on direct personal observations, not assumptions.

Restaurant: Gepuklah·Cuisine: Ayam Gepuk·1,000–1,200 words required

This is a suitable topic because it connects to my interest in Malaysian food and I have a photo of the meal. The final review needs to discuss what I actually experienced, including what worked well and any limitations.

Photo requirement: The assignment brief asks for at least three self-taken pictures of you eating and sampling dishes at the restaurant/F&B establishment. The current food-only photo does not satisfy this requirement on its own. Plan to collect the required photos at the establishment.

1. Introduction

Introduce the establishment, location, visit context and reason for choosing the dish. Add the date/location details from your own visit.

2. Presentation and food quality

Describe the meal's appearance, colours, arrangement, ingredients visible and portion presentation. Avoid treating the photograph alone as proof of flavour.

3. Sensory critique

To complete: your real observations about taste, aroma, texture, sambal heat, seasoning and how the components work together.

4. Dining experience and context

Add verified details about location, ambiance, service and price/value where you remember them. Cuisine background or culinary context may be included with reliable sources if relevant.

5. Recommendation and conclusion

Offer a balanced verdict: what stood out, who might enjoy the dish, and what a potential diner should know.
