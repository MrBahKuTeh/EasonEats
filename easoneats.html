02 / My original foodie posts

Welcome to EasonEats

Malaysian food, pizza and fast food — three different cravings, one curious eater. These posts use my own food photos and a personal food-media perspective.

What does EasonEats mean?

EasonEats is my foodie persona: a place to share food adventures, pay attention to flavour and presentation, and show how meals become part of everyday life and social connection. Being a foodie is not only about eating; it also means observing, describing, discovering and sharing food experiences with others.

Malaysian food · Gepuklah

01. A Sambal-Lover's Kind of Meal 🌶️

Ayam gepuk caught my eye with its golden fried chicken, bright sambal, egg and side dishes. The mix of colours makes the meal feel generous and full of character.

I used a high-angle close-up to show the different components together. I wanted the viewer to notice the contrast between the chicken, red sambal and the other sides.

My foodie perspective: I enjoy exploring how a meal brings together different textures, colours and flavours. A good food photo should make people curious about the experience, not just the dish's name.

Pizza · Pizza Mansion

02. One Table, Too Many Good Choices 🍕

A table filled with pizzas and pasta captures the fun of eating together and having choices. Rather than focusing on just one plate, this photo shows the whole spread.

I used a wider, slightly elevated angle to include several dishes in one frame. This gives the photo a shared-meal feeling and lets viewers compare the different dishes.

My foodie perspective: Exploring food also means being open to different combinations and enjoying the social side of dining. Food can be a conversation starter as much as something we eat.

Fast food · Shake Shack

03. Comfort Food, No Complicated Plans 🍔

This tray brings together burgers, fries and drinks in a clear, colourful arrangement. The variety makes the meal easy to read at a glance.

The elevated three-quarter angle shows both the tops and sides of the burgers while keeping the fries in view. The tray frames the items and pulls them together.

My foodie perspective: Fast food is also part of today's food scene. Branding, convenience, presentation and personal expectations can influence how people experience a meal.
