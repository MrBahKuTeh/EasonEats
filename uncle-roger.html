Why I chose Uncle Roger

I chose Uncle Roger because he makes food content entertaining through humour, strong reactions and commentary on cooking techniques. His videos invite viewers to think about how food is prepared while making the subject accessible to a wide online audience.

His content is especially interesting for a food-media assignment because it combines comedy, cultural identity, online trends and audience participation.
